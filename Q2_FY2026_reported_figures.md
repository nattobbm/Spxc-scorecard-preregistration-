## Context recorded at the 2026-07-23 baseline, relevant to F3 scoring

The 2026-07-23 research baseline (pre-earnings) noted: Section 232 tariff impact of $0.05–0.10 EPS, ~75–80% concentrated in Q2 HVAC, plus capacity ramp start-up costs of ~$8–9M for the full year. The 10-Q MD&A attributes the Q2 HVAC margin decline to: (i) start-up costs and related inefficiencies from capacity expansion initiatives, (ii) net tariff headwinds and inflationary cost increases, (iii) a favorable mix in the prior-year period. Citing these at scoring time is not post-hoc reinterpretation; they were recorded before the print.
