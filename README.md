What this is, and what it is not
It is a judgment record: four numeric failure conditions, frozen and published while the outcome is unknown, to be scored PASS/FAIL against reported numbers with no post-hoc reinterpretation. Wins and losses get published identically.
It is not statistical evidence (n = 1), not investment advice, and not a claim of skill. Only an accumulated series of such records means anything.
