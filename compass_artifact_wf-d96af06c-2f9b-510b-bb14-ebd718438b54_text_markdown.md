# SPX Technologies (NYSE: SPXC) — Pre-Earnings Factual Baseline for Q2 FY2026 (Report After Close July 30, 2026)

*Facts compiled with explicit measurement bases (口径). No investment conclusions, no threshold recommendations, no strong/weak characterizations. Conflicts and data gaps flagged inline.*

**TL;DR**
- HVAC backlog rose from $436.8M (Dec 31, 2024) to a record $755.3M (Mar 28, 2026); the Q1 FY2026 figure was reported as "+38% organically YoY." The company discloses backlog organic-vs-acquisition splits inconsistently, so HVAC organic **revenue** growth (Q1 FY2026: +9.6%) is the cleanest recurring proxy where the backlog dollar split is not given.
- Segment margins diverged in Q1 FY2026: HVAC segment margin fell 40 bps YoY to 22.5% (start-up costs from capacity expansion), while D&M segment margin rose 410 bps YoY to 27.0% (high-margin software mix, partly one-time). Management quantifies data-center **guidance** — per CEO Gene Lowe on the Apr 30, 2026 call, growth was moved "from somewhere around 50% to 70%" for 2026, lifting the revenue base "from $200 million to an estimated $350 million" — rather than reporting an actual data-center revenue line item.
- Q2 FY2026 consensus clusters at $1.85–$1.89 adjusted EPS and ~$640–$652M revenue; per MarketBeat (via Daily Political, Jul 23, 2026), "analysts expect SPX Technologies to announce earnings of $1.85 per share and revenue of $640.3420 million for the quarter." FY2026 company guidance is adjusted EPS $7.75–$8.15 (midpoint $7.95), revenue $2.575–$2.645B. Stock closed $212.86 on July 20, 2026, ~14% below a June 22 all-time closing high of $246.41.

- **Organic vs. acquisition disclosure:** SPX gives an organic backlog growth label for some quarters (Q4 FY2025 "+22% organically," Q1 FY2026 "+38% organically") but does NOT publish a recurring organic-vs-acquisition **dollar** split for backlog. The 10-K discloses acquisition-attributable backlog only annually (Sigma & Omega = $51.3M of the Dec 31, 2025 HVAC backlog).
- **Proxy where the split is absent — HVAC organic revenue growth by quarter:** Q2 FY2025 +0.7% (or ~7% excluding a large prior-year cooling service project); Q3 FY2025 +9.0%; Q4 FY2025 +10.3%; Q1 FY2026 +9.6%.
- **Data gap flagged:** No quarter-by-quarter organic backlog **dollar** figure is published. Use the organic % labels from calls plus organic revenue growth as the proxy.

### 3. Data Center Exposure
*(口径: management gives data-center revenue GUIDANCE and growth rates, not a reported actual data-center revenue line item.)*

- **Q4 FY2025 call (Feb 24, 2026):** CEO Lowe: "2025 would be about 9% of revenue… in the neighborhood of $200,000,000… We would anticipate that to be… low double digits, say, 12%… probably in the 50% neighborhood for our data centers going into 2026." Guided ~50% growth to ~12% of total sales.
- **Q1 FY2026 call (Apr 30, 2026) — guidance RAISED:** CEO Lowe: "With the change in outlook this year, we moved our data center growth from somewhere around 50% to 70%." Per the Motley Fool transcript key metric, this increased "the revenue base from $200 million to an estimated $350 million." CFO Carano: demand "very strong, and we would say accelerating… we actually see some attractive runway looking ahead in 2027 and 2028." HVAC backlog acceleration "driven almost entirely by data center demand."
- **Capacity:** Management stated the expansions "give us the ability to serve… circa $550 million of incremental data center revenue" at full build-out. (Note a third-party framing: TIKR states the three facilities "together with existing infrastructure give SPX Technologies the ability to serve approximately $750 million of data center revenue at full build-out" — **flag: $550M is management's stated incremental figure; $750M is TIKR's total-capacity characterization.**)
- **Facilities:** Olathe, Kansas; Tennessee (TAMCO aluminum dampers); Madison/Huntsville, Alabama (459,000 sq ft, OlympusMAX). Olathe and Tennessee producing/ramping; Madison assembly capability H2 2026, initial production H1 2027. SPX is investing more than $100 million to expand HVAC manufacturing capacity (per Trefis, cited June 10, 2026).
- **Quantification status:** Data-center revenue is quantified only via guidance ($350M target for 2026, ~70% YoY growth); actual quarterly data-center revenue is NOT broken out in filings.

- **13F check (Aug 14, 2026):** compare Q2 2026 filings against the Q1 2026 active-manager baseline above — Capital Group entities (Capital International Investors, Capital World Investors) and First Trust were the largest Q1 adds; Jane Street (near-exit), T. Benchmarks that would materially change the picture: an organic HVAC backlog deceleration, an HVAC segment margin below the FY guide floor (24.5%), a data-center guide held flat at $350M (vs. raised), or net active-manager selling in the Q2 13Fs.

- No Morningstar-specific SPXC fair value estimate was located; GuruFocus GF Value and Simply Wall St DCF are model-driven substitutes.
- SPX labels fiscal quarters with period-end dates near calendar quarter-ends (e.g., Q1 FY2026 ended Mar 28, 2026); "FY2026" tracks calendar 2026. Q2 FY2026 covers the quarter ended ~June 27, 2026, to be reported after close July 30, 2026.
